Educator Outreach Platform

NorthNode Educator Outreach is an internal tool built by NorthNode Technologies to help our team send personalized academic outreach emails to professors and educators through connected personal Gmail accounts.

This tool is not a public product. It is used exclusively by NorthNode Technologies team members for outreach operations.

Gmail OAuth 2.0

Team members connect their personal Gmail accounts via official Google authorization. No passwords are stored.

Personalized Outreach

Send individually personalized emails to educators and professors. Each email is sent one-to-one.

Data Privacy

No email content is shared externally. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. See our Privacy Policy.
